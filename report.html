Somis Nut House: review report

This report covers two separate jobs. Phase 1 replaces Rebecca's existing WordPress site. Phase 2 is the AI demo with the animated squirrel. They are kept apart on purpose. Nothing was sent to Rebecca, and the live somisnuthouse.com site was not touched. Back to the checklist

Phase 1: fast replacement of Rebecca's site

The demo Rebecca saw is at eugene-elder.github.io/somis-redesign-demo. It's a public GitHub Pages link, so no sign-in is needed. Somis Squirrel Video built it on October 24.

What Eugene provided: the creation brief and five-screen ask; the project archive, with the address, phone, email, the "Nuts About Flavor" tagline, palette, typography, the home, shop, cart and gift-box plans, Butter Toffee Almonds prices, taglines and the mascot; his approval of regenerated art; and the robots, iOS and GitHub Pages fixes.

What the bot filled in: a $50 free-shipping threshold and cart wording; gift-box sizes (4, 6 and 9 compartments) with ribbon options and a sample note; a bigger catalog with placeholder prices, 4.9-star ratings and sample reviews; a 12-product shop with filter chips; eight AI product photos, an SVG squirrel and a hero loop; the phone-frame look and navigation; a "Demo only" checkout; and exact fonts and colors built around the palette. It did not use the Phase 2 spec, a live-site scrape, or other bots' notes.

Phase 2: AI demo and animated squirrel

The live demo is at eugene-elder.github.io/somis-demo (free GitHub Pages, no sign-in). The flow goes like this: tap to start, an antique brass rotary phone rings, and the squirrel answers with the welcome line. Allowing the mic leads to the voice call. Declining goes to the normal shop.

Somis Site Builder: built the site structure; the squirrel call page with Rebecca's V1 slide-up cart, which ends at $27.93 for 2 lb of Salted 320ct Cashews ($8.99 each, SKU 328) plus $9.95 shipping, as a gift for grandma by Nov 20; a normal-site shop with 187 real products grouped from a read-only pull of 262 items on the public product list; a demo checkout; and an admin mock-up for Rebecca covering the welcome line, photos and inventory counts. Data issues found: the Salted Macadamia description says unsalted, about 35 products have no size, and some prices don't scale.

Somis Squirrel Animator: built the 3D Pixar-style squirrel, going from v3 to v4, with v5 in progress. It lip-syncs to the pre-recorded Poppy voice line "Hi, this is Somnus Nut House. What can I do for you today?" The line can be swapped for Rebecca's voice later. The animator's own choices were Piper text-to-speech, Rhubarb lip-sync, a cream apron with an acorn, earthy colors, 15 frames per second and a homemade ring sound. The squirrel clip won't be rebuilt until Eugene says so.

Somis Demo Screens: made the fake Windows mic prompt and the fake card payment page, which uses no real card data: a sample 4242 card and order #DEMO-0001, showing the $27.93 total. Eugene's only direct instruction to them was to pick an easy-to-animate rotary phone image from his Google Images search. The phone photo comes from Wikimedia and needs attribution.

Somis Squirrel Video: made the squirrel hop video loop and the Phase 1 demo above.

Pricing: v1 is $2,000 (agreed). Phase 2 is about $8,000, Eugene's rough estimate.

Still open: the background image from the mock-up Rebecca saw; fixes to the product data; 13 spec questions in site-plan.md; and a test of the real iPhone mic prompt.
